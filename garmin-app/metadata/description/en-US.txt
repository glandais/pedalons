Your Pédalons teams' rides and routes on your Edge.

Pédalons (pedalons.fr) is a free platform for cycling clubs and groups: rides, routes, pace groups. This app is its companion for Garmin Edge bike computers.

• Your teams' rides, from the day before to the week ahead, with each group's start time and the route attached to it.
• Your teams' routes that belong to no ride, nearest first.
• One press downloads a route: it joins the Edge's courses, ready to navigate.
• Distance and elevation in kilometres or miles, following the Edge's settings.
• English and French interface.

Pairing without a keyboard: the Edge shows a QR code and a six-character code. Scan it with your phone or go to pedalons.fr/garmin, sign in and check the code. To unpair the Edge: "Paired devices" in your profile on the website ("Logout", in the app, only clears the session from the Edge).

What you need:
• a Pédalons account (free) and at least one team;
• an Edge connected to your phone through the Garmin Connect app, which gives it Internet access.

Location: when it opens, the app sends the Edge's position to pedalons.fr to sort routes by distance. It is not stored. Privacy policy: https://www.pedalons.fr/privacy
