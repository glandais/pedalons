## 2. Service Description

Pedalons is a cycling team management platform that allows you to:

- Create and manage teams
- Plan and share bike rides and multi-day trips
- Import, draw, analyse and share GPS routes, and export them as GPX or FIT files
- Publish content (posts, comments)
- Post classified listings within your team (items for sale, for rent, or wanted), when enabled for your team. Pedalons only hosts the listing: it is not a party to any sale or rental, does not process payments, and does not verify listings or items
- Subscribe to your rides and trips from an external calendar application, using a personal link. Anyone who holds this link can read these events without logging in: do not share it, and regenerate it if it has circulated
- Connect GPS devices and services (Hammerhead Karoo, Garmin, Wahoo) and send your routes to them
- Receive notifications about your teams' activity, in the app and, depending on your settings, by email or push. A team's administrators can also relay its announcements to an external chat service, such as Slack or Discord
- Invite people to your team by email, if you are one of its administrators. Only invite people who expect to hear from your team. Nobody becomes a member without accepting the invitation

Depending on your team's settings, other members may see the team's member list (names and pictures, and, if the team opens its member directory, roles and join dates).

A file analysed with the GPX tool is reachable by anyone who has its link, even without an account, so only share that link with people you trust.
