Pedalons is the private space of a cycling club: its rides (with pace groups to sign up for), its route library, its calendar, its posts and its members' classified ads. Everything happens inside a team; a member only sees the teams they belong to.

SIGNING IN
Use the demo account given in the review information, with its password (e-mail + password on the sign-in screen). The account is a member of a team that already holds rides, routes and posts, so every screen has content.

WHAT TO LOOK AT
- Home: the next ride you are registered for, then the team feed.
- A ride: pick a pace group and register, then unregister.
- Routes: list and map views (Explore opens the map of every route), a route's elevation profile with its surface and its points, GPX/FIT download.
- Calendar, posts and their comments, classified ads.
- A team: its dashboard (your next rides, latest posts, new routes, ads), then its Agenda, Posts, Routes, Ads and Members.
- A ride's weather, once it is less than seven days away: the "Weather at the start" card under the date; then tap a pace group's card to open its page, whose Weather tab gives rain, temperature and headwind or tailwind along the route at that group's pace, also drawn on its map.
- Profile: account settings, and account deletion under Profile > My account > Danger zone.

PERMISSIONS
- Notifications are never requested at launch: the prompt appears only when the member turns push notifications on from the Notifications inbox.
- Location is requested only when the member turns on the "around me" filter, at low accuracy, and is never stored.
- Photo library access is used only to pick a profile picture.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
Content is published inside a team and visible to its members; a post, ride or trip can also be made public by its author. The demo account is a plain member, and every step below works from it.
- Report a comment: open a ride from Home, scroll to Comments, tap "⋯" on another member's comment > Report > pick a reason > Send. The comment disappears from your view at once.
- Report a post, ride, trip or ad: open it, tap "⋯" in the top bar > Report > reason > Send. On a route, "⋯" is the round button at the top right of the map.
- Block a user: "⋯" on one of their comments (or on their ad) > Block {name} > Block. Their comments, posts and ads disappear from your view; they are not told. Unblock under Profile > Privacy > Blocked users.
- Reports reach the team's organizers and administrators (push notification and inbox) and the Pedalons team, through a moderation queue where content is removed or kept. Content reported by three members is hidden from everyone until a moderator decides. We act on reports within 24 hours.
- Objectionable terms are refused at publication, and the Terms of Service, accepted with a required checkbox at sign-up, set zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.
Anyone can also reach us at contact@pedalons.fr (https://www.pedalons.fr/support).

GPS DEVICES
The "Send to device" action on a route appears only once a Hammerhead Karoo or Garmin bike computer has been paired from Profile > Devices and services, which needs the physical device.
