Pédalons is the home of your cycling club or group: rides, routes, the agenda and team news, all in one place.

RIDES
• Every ride offers its pace groups: sign up for yours in one tap, and see who is riding with whom.
• Start location and time, planned route, comments: everything you need to know before heading out.
• The ride's weather in the week before it: at the start, then along the route at your group's pace — rain, temperature, and headwind or tailwind, point by point.
• Multi-day trips, stage by stage, with the weather for each stage.

ROUTES
• The club's route library, on the map or as a list, filtered by distance and elevation.
• An elevation profile coloured by gradient, and the climbs along the way.
• Download as GPX or FIT, and send a route to your Hammerhead Karoo or Garmin bike computer.

THE TEAM
• Each team's dashboard: your next rides, the latest posts, new routes and ads.
• The agenda of rides and trips, as a list or a calendar, which you can subscribe to from your own calendar app.
• Club posts, to keep everyone in the loop.
• Classified ads between members, without your e-mail address ever being shown.
• Notifications for ride reminders, schedule changes and replies to your comments, set type by type.

A Pédalons account is created with an e-mail address; sign-in works with a password, a one-time code or passkeys.
